HP ZGX Nano G1n

NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GB10 in HP’s ZGX Nano: 128GB unified LPDDR5X, DGX OS, ConnectX dual 200GbE class networking, and HP Z Toolkit / channel support. Same silicon class as the DGX Spark and Lenovo ThinkStation PGX; different invoice.

What it is

HP’s ZGX Nano G1n is a deskside GB10 appliance in the same Grace Blackwell class as the NVIDIA DGX Spark: Arm + Blackwell on one package, 128GB coherent LPDDR5X at roughly 273 GB/s, M.2 storage options, 10GbE plus dual 200GbE-class ConnectX networking, and NVIDIA DGX OS. Form factor is nano SFF. You buy it through HP’s Z / workstation channel and Z Toolkit ecosystem rather than NVIDIA Founders or another partner badge.

Who it is for

CUDA people who already live in HP Z procurement, want enterprise channel paperwork, or need HP support on a 128GB unified GB10 desk box. Wrong if you only want the cheapest GB10 SKU or a Windows-first mini.

What it actually runs

  • Comfortable: 20B–70B dense and most 120B-class MoE models, same capacity story as other GB10 cubes.
  • Possible: larger models when two units are paired over the high-speed fabric (~256GB coherent class). Quantized 200B-class depends on context.
  • Not the win: beating a discrete high-bandwidth card on tokens-per-second for models that already fit in VRAM. Capacity is the product; 273 GB/s is the ceiling.

Standout details

  • Price snapshot Sep 2026: street listings for configured units commonly land around ~$6,030–$7,400 depending on storage (4TB configs skew higher). Confirm the exact HP SKU and support options before checkout—channel quotes move.
  • Same GB10 silicon and bandwidth class as the NVIDIA DGX Spark, Dell Pro Max with GB10, ASUS Ascent GX10, and Lenovo ThinkStation PGX. Buy HP for Z channel and toolkit, not new silicon.
  • Memory is soldered. Storage is M.2 class. Networking matches the GB10 partner pattern: 10GbE plus dual 200GbE-class ConnectX ports for clustering.
  • DGX OS and the NVIDIA developer stack are the path. Windows is not.

Honest verdict

Buy the HP ZGX Nano G1n if HP Z channel, Z Toolkit, or an HP support number matters and you need the GB10 128GB capacity tier. Skip it if a cheaper complete Spark / GX10 / Dell / Lenovo config is available and you do not need the HP invoice. Same silicon, same bandwidth limiter.
