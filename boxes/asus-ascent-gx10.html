What it is

ASUS’s GB10 workstation in the DGX Spark class: 128GB coherent memory, Blackwell-generation on-package GPU, CUDA as the native language.

Who it is for

Buyers who want the Spark workflow and do not need the Founders Edition on the invoice.

What it actually runs

  • Same model tiers as Spark: 20B–70B dense, 120B-class MoE.
  • Same limiter as Spark: memory bandwidth, not the TOPS slide.

Honest verdict

If you have already decided on GB10, shop Spark and GX10 as one product with two invoices. Pick the cheaper complete config you can actually get support on.
