GIGABYTE AI TOP ATOM

NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GB10 in a 150 × 150 × 50.5 mm cube with 128GB unified LPDDR5X, DGX OS, and GIGABYTE AI TOP Utility. Same silicon as the DGX Spark; different invoice and channel.

What it is

GIGABYTE’s partner take on the GB10 Superchip: 20 Arm cores (10× Cortex-X925 + 10× Cortex-A725), Blackwell GPU with 6,144 CUDA cores, 128GB coherent LPDDR5X at 273 GB/s, up to 4 TB M.2, ConnectX-7 SmartNIC, 10GbE, Wi-Fi 7, HDMI 2.1a, four USB-C (one PD-in), and a ~240W adapter. Ships with NVIDIA DGX OS (Ubuntu-based) and the CUDA/NGC stack, plus GIGABYTE’s AI TOP Utility for local model download, inference, and RAG. Footprint matches the Spark: 150 × 150 × ~50.5 mm, ~1.2 kg. Two units can be linked over the ConnectX-7 for roughly double the memory pool and models up to ~405B parameters.

Who it is for

CUDA people who already buy through GIGABYTE or need the AI TOP channel and utility, and want 128GB unified memory on a desk without chasing the NVIDIA Founders Edition or another partner SKU. Wrong if you just want the cheapest GB10 cube or a Windows-first box.

What it actually runs

  • Comfortable: 20B–70B dense and most 120B-class MoE models, same capacity story as Spark.
  • Possible: larger models when two units are paired (~256GB coherent). Quantized 200B-class fits on one unit depending on context.
  • Not the win: beating a discrete 32GB card on tokens-per-second for anything that already fits in VRAM. Bandwidth is 273 GB/s; capacity is the product.

Standout details

  • Price snapshot Sep 2026: Newegg 1TB configs from ~$4,300; 4TB models $5,999–$6,600+ at B&H, CDW, and resellers. Japan street ~¥670k earlier; Europe €5.1k–€6.8k. Confirm the exact SKU (ATAGB10-9000 / 9001 / 9002) before checkout—prices move with storage.
  • Same GB10 silicon and 273 GB/s ceiling as the NVIDIA DGX Spark, Acer Veriton GN100, Dell Pro Max, HP ZGX Nano, and Lenovo ThinkStation PGX. Buy this for GIGABYTE channel or the AI TOP Utility, not new silicon.
  • Memory is soldered. Storage is a single M.2 (1–4 TB Gen4/Gen5 options). No upgrade path beyond a second box.
  • DGX OS and the NVIDIA developer stack ship ready. Windows is not the path.

Honest verdict

Buy the GIGABYTE AI TOP ATOM if GIGABYTE support or the AI TOP Utility matters and you need the GB10 128GB capacity tier. Skip it if a cheaper Spark or GX10 SKU is available and you do not need the GIGABYTE invoice. Same silicon, same bandwidth limiter. Side-by-side class: Spark / GX10 / Acer Veriton GN100.
