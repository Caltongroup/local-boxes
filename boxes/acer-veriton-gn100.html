Acer Veriton GN100

NVIDIA Grace Blackwell GB10 in a 150 × 150 × 51 mm cube with 128GB unified LPDDR5X, DGX OS, and Acer support. Same silicon as the DGX Spark; different invoice and channel.

What it is

Acer’s partner take on the GB10 Superchip: 20 Arm cores (10× Cortex-X925 + 10× Cortex-A725), Blackwell GPU with 6,144 CUDA cores, 128GB coherent LPDDR5X at 273 GB/s, up to 4 TB M.2, ConnectX-7 SmartNIC, Wi-Fi 7, HDMI 2.1, four USB-C (one PD-in), and a ~240W adapter. Ships with NVIDIA DGX OS (Ubuntu-based) and the CUDA/NGC stack. Footprint matches the Spark: 150 × 150 × ~51 mm, ~1.2 kg. Two units can be linked over the ConnectX-7 for roughly double the memory pool and models up to ~405B parameters.

Who it is for

CUDA people who already buy through Acer or need the Veriton channel and support options, and want 128GB unified memory on a desk without chasing the NVIDIA Founders Edition or another partner SKU. Wrong if you just want the cheapest GB10 cube or a Windows-first box.

What it actually runs

  • Comfortable: 20B–70B dense and most 120B-class MoE models, same capacity story as Spark.
  • Possible: larger models when two units are paired (~256GB coherent). Quantized 200B-class fits on one unit depending on context.
  • Not the win: beating a discrete 32GB card on tokens-per-second for anything that already fits in VRAM. Bandwidth is 273 GB/s; capacity is the product.

Standout details

  • Price snapshot Sep 2026: Acer list historically $3,999; street sightings $3,700–$5,500 depending on channel and storage (1TB configs lower when available; 4TB more common). Best Buy and CDW listings have floated in the low $4k range. Confirm the exact SKU before checkout—prices move with memory and storage.
  • Same GB10 silicon and 273 GB/s ceiling as the NVIDIA DGX Spark, Dell Pro Max, HP ZGX Nano, and Lenovo ThinkStation PGX. Buy this for Acer channel or procurement rules, not new silicon.
  • Memory is soldered. Storage is a single M.2 (1–4 TB options; access varies by SKU). No upgrade path beyond a second box.
  • DGX OS and the NVIDIA developer stack ship ready. Windows is not the path.

Honest verdict

Buy the Acer Veriton GN100 if Acer support or channel matters and you need the GB10 128GB capacity tier. Skip it if a cheaper Spark or GX10 SKU is available and you do not need the Acer invoice. Same silicon, same bandwidth limiter. Side-by-side class: Spark / GX10 / Dell Pro Max.
