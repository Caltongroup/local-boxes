Mac Studio M5 Max / M5 Ultra

What it is

The 2026 Mac Studio, announced 25 Aug 2026. Same chassis shape as prior Studios. M5 Max: 18-core CPU, up to 40-core GPU with Neural Accelerators, up to 128GB unified memory, up to 614 GB/s bandwidth. M5 Ultra: up to 36-core CPU, up to 80-core GPU, up to 512GB unified memory, 1.2 TB/s bandwidth. Storage is soldered SSD (512GB–8TB on Max; 1TB–16TB on Ultra). Thunderbolt 5, Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6. Ships with macOS 27.

Who it is for

macOS users who want high unified memory for local LLMs, video, or pro workloads and already live in Apple’s stack. Good when capacity and silence matter more than CUDA or repairability. Wrong if you need discrete GPU memory clocks, ROCm/CUDA tooling, or an upgrade path after purchase.

What it actually runs

  • Comfortable: 14B–70B dense and many MoE models on 64–128GB Max configs; larger frontier-class models once you have 256GB+ Ultra memory. Capacity is the product.
  • Possible: 200B-class and higher on the 512GB Ultra SKU (late October). Bandwidth on Ultra (1.2 TB/s) is the highest Apple has shipped in this form factor.
  • Not the win: CUDA-native stacks, training that assumes NVIDIA tools, or anything that needs more than the soldered ceiling. Independent tok/s numbers for M5 Max/Ultra are not yet public as of 19 Sep 2026; do not invent them.

Standout details

  • Price snapshot 19 Sep 2026: Apple list M5 Max from $2,499 (36GB/512GB); M5 Ultra from $5,499 (96GB/1TB). Configured 128GB Max builds land in the mid-to-high $5k range; maxed Ultra configs (256GB now, 512GB later) climb well past $10k. Education pricing $200–$400 lower on base SKUs. Confirm the exact config on apple.com before checkout.
  • Availability: pre-order open since 25 Aug. Customer deliveries and store stock begin 22 Sep 2026. The 512GB unified-memory Ultra configuration is delayed until late October.
  • Memory and storage are soldered. No later upgrade path. Buy the RAM you need on day one.
  • Not CUDA. MLX, llama.cpp, Ollama, and LM Studio are the practical local paths on macOS. Thunderbolt 5 clustering is mentioned by Apple for multi-box inference; treat that as a future option, not a shipping feature set you can measure today.

Honest verdict

Buy the Mac Studio M5 if you already live on macOS, need 128GB+ unified capacity, and can wait for 22 Sep (or late October for 512GB). Skip it if you need CUDA, open hardware, or a box that ships this week — look at Spark / GX10 / Dell Pro Max for the GB10 128GB tier, or Framework / Halo cubes for Strix Halo. Capacity is real; the shipping date and Apple memory tax are the catches.
