What it is

Minisforum’s flagship AI NAS: Ryzen AI Max+ 395 (16 Zen 5 cores, Radeon 8060S), 64GB or 128GB of soldered LPDDR5X on a 256-bit bus, five 3.5/2.5-inch SATA bays, five M.2 NVMe slots (one occupied by the 128GB system SSD), dual 10GbE, dual USB4 v2 (80 Gbps), HDMI 2.1, and a built-in 250W PSU. Runs MinisCloud OS (with Windows 11 Pro / Linux options). Dimensions roughly 199 × 202 × 252 mm, about 5.8 kg. Same Strix Halo silicon as the MS-S1 MAX and the sealed cubes, packaged as a networked storage appliance instead of a desk mini.

Who it is for

People who want a 24B–70B (or larger MoE on 128GB) local model living next to a serious storage array and dual 10GbE. Home labs, small teams, or anyone who already thinks in NAS form factors. Wrong if you want a quiet Windows desk cube you can shove under a monitor, or if your tools assume CUDA only.

What it actually runs

  • Comfortable: 24B–32B dense at usable speed on the iGPU via llama.cpp / ROCm / Vulkan.
  • Possible: 70B-class dense and larger MoE when you buy the 128GB config and accept that ~256 GB/s is the limiter. Capacity is the win; decode is not Mac Studio or discrete-VRAM class.
  • Not the win: training, CUDA-only stacks, or treating the five HDD bays as free headroom for models that still have to fit in the soldered unified memory.

Standout details

  • Price snapshot Sep 2026, Minisforum US store: 64GB + 128GB OS SSD ~$2,399 (sale from ~$2,999); 128GB configs have tracked around $3,599 (sale from higher list). Confirm the exact SKU before checkout—store bullets and dropdowns have mixed the two.
  • Memory is soldered LPDDR5X (default ~8000 MT/s, up to 8533 MT/s OC) on a 256-bit bus. ~256 GB/s. No upgrade path. Pick 128GB now if large models are the point.
  • Five SATA + five M.2 (one used by system SSD) for hybrid capacity up to the claimed ~200TB class. Dual 10GbE (RTL8127) and dual USB4 v2 are the networking differentiators.
  • MinisCloud OS is the primary path, with Docker one-click and a local AI agent story. Windows 11 Pro and Linux are supported if you want a conventional stack.
  • Same chip class as Framework Desktop, MS-S1 MAX, EVO-X2/X3, and the sealed cubes. Buy this for the bays and dual 10GbE in one chassis; buy MS-S1 MAX if you want a more desk-oriented Halo with a limited PCIe slot instead of five HDD trays.

Honest verdict

Buy the 128GB N5 MAX if you actually need a 5-bay NAS that also runs 24B–70B-class models locally and dual 10GbE is non-negotiable. Skip the 64GB config for capacity work. Same ~256 GB/s ceiling as every other Max+ 395 box. This is not a desk Windows cube—form factor and OS expectations differ. If you want repairable Halo, get Framework Desktop. If you want CUDA capacity, look at Spark / GX10.
