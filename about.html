About Local Boxes

Local Boxes is a slow directory of computers that run real models on a desk. We list what the machine is, who it is for, what it actually runs, and the catch.

It exists because the roundups lie with benchmarks and hide the wall: soldered memory, 32GB of GDDR, a $249 kit that is a camera computer and not a ChatGPT replacement. Verdicts here can be negative. That is the product.

Calton Group, LLC publishes it. We do not stock machines. We do not take a cut until the site has real traffic. Prices are USD snapshots because memory markets move. If a card is wrong, write. If you want a box that is not here yet, it is probably in the queue.

Curated directory, not a review mill. Affiliate and sponsor outreach stay off until traffic is real.
