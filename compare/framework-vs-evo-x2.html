Who should buy Framework Desktop

You want the cheaper entry into this exact silicon, you do not mind building it, and you want a machine you can open and repair in five years instead of replacing.

Who should buy GMKtec EVO-X2

You want the same chip already built into a real Windows PC today, you do not want to assemble anything, and the ~$300–400 premium over a comparable Framework config is worth not touching a screwdriver. Get the 128GB SKU — the $2,199 listing is the 64GB config.
