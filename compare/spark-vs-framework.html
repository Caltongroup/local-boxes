Who should buy Spark

You already live in CUDA, need the NVIDIA path for tooling that assumes it, and want a turnkey 128GB brick that can load models a 32GB card cannot. Capacity and software continuity matter more than tokens-per-dollar or repairability.

Who should buy Framework Desktop

You want one machine for daily work and local models, prefer open/repairable hardware, and can live with ROCm or Vulkan instead of CUDA. The midrange Strix Halo pick when you assemble it yourself and keep the parts list honest.
