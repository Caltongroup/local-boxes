AMD Ryzen AI Halo vs ASUS ROG NUC 16

Not the same job. Halo is a 128GB unified Strix Halo cube for ~70B capacity on ROCm / llama.cpp / Vulkan — not CUDA. ROG NUC 16 is a 3L laptop-RTX CUDA mini: fast while the model fits in 16GB VRAM, then the wall. You also pay a gaming tax.

Who should buy Halo

You want 128GB unified capacity for 24B–70B locally, you can live without CUDA, and you will pay Micro Center list for AMD’s official Strix Halo developer box. Wrong if you need NVIDIA tooling, the cheapest Max+ 395, or a store that ships.

Who should buy ROG NUC 16

You want a compact CUDA gaming mini that also runs 8B–14B well, and you already live in the NVIDIA stack. Wrong if 70B capacity is the job.
