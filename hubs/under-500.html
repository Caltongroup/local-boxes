Jetson and boxes under $500

Cheap local AI is almost all Jetson. This hub only lists machines that run models on-device without a cloud bill and cost under $500 new.

What under $500 can and cannot mean

At this price you get an edge computer, not a 70B workstation. Memory is the wall. 8GB unified memory can hold a small language model or a camera stack. It cannot hold Spark-class weights.
