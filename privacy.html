Privacy

Calton Group, LLC publishes Local Boxes at localboxes.org. There are no accounts, no checkout, and no newsletter on this site today.

What we collect

Cloudflare Web Analytics runs on these pages. It tells us that a page was visited, roughly from where, and on what kind of browser. It is there so “real traffic” is a number, not a feeling. We do not use it to build a dossier on you.

If you email admin@caltongroup.com, we have whatever you put in that message. We use it to answer you. We do not sell, rent, or trade an email list, because we do not keep one for marketing.

What we do not collect

We do not take payment information. We do not ask you to create a login. We do not run a cookie-consent wall, because we are not running an ad network on this site.

If that changes

Affiliate or sponsor links stay off until traffic is real. If we ever add them, or a mailing list, this page will say so before the footer does. Until then, treat this as a directory with a traffic counter.

Questions: admin@caltongroup.com.
